Lord Aurora (オーロラ様?) is a Wind Clan fairy acting as Head of the Wind Clan (風の氏族の長?) and Lord of Salisbury (ソールズベリーの領主?) in the British Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

BACKGROUND

Aurora is the leader of the Wind Clan. She is also the leader of Salisbury, and her assistant is Coral. Melusine, who she found among the remains of Albion, is her retainer.

With the prophecy of the oncoming Calamity, Queen Morgan offered Aurora safety in Camelot. Aurora said she would only come if the people of Salisbury could come as well, and so Morgan rejected her and forbade her entry.

PERSONALITY

Aurora loves new and interesting things. She desires equality between humans and fairies. However beneath her kindly surface she is the "perfect fairy": fickle, narcissistic, unable to understand the concepts of right and wrong, and completely incapable of long-term planning. The only thing she truly cares about is being loved by others, and she is completely oblivious to the wanton destruction she causes as a result of this. Her need to be in the "spotlight" led her, through her followers, to eliminate those who would steal it from her, such was the case with Ainsel and Uther. She also has no tolerance for those who irritate her, as she doesn't think twice before killing Coral despite the fairy's loyalty. She takes everything her subjects do for her, for granted, as seen by her treatment of Melusine. Only after being fatally wounded by Melusine and seeing her fly in her dragon form did she realize her action of taking her in is perhaps the one good thing she had ever done in her life.

Oberon calls her his exact opposite, as while everything he says becomes a lie, everything she says becomes truth. No matter how dark her emotions are, no matter how she pretends to be something she isn't, the instant she says it, everyone, the world, and even herself, will believe it to be true without a shred of lie to it. According to him, Aurora doesn't even realise she's lying to herself, and that the reason she's survived so long is because she is both the perpetrator and the bystander to her own lies; not the most beautiful fairy in Britain, but the most innocent usurper in Britain.

FAE ROUND TABLE DOMAIN: AVALON LE FAE

Artoria once tried to speak with the head of the Wind Clan, Aurora, the last time she visited Salisbury, but she was turned away at the door. Oberon says he can get Artoria an audience with Aurora though.

Norwich is the only place Aurora cannot contact her clan, but they will still help in the search for Mash Kyrielight. She tells Ritsuka Fujimaru to give Coral a description of the someone they’re looking for. Oberon tells her that Artoria is the Child of Prophecy. He asks Aurora to give Artoria her approval by letting Artoria ring Salisbury’s bell. Aurora refuses to allow it until she is provided proof of Artoria’s worth as king. Then asking Artoria and Ritsuka for a sign of hope, she says the Calamity Pool, a powerful Mors Current, began engulfing Norwich several months ago. If Artoria and Ritsuka can cleanse it, she will give them Salisbury’s bell.

As part of a deal with Oberon, Koyanskaya kills the reinforcements Morgan sent to Woodwose. Aurora takes this opportunity to lie to Woodwose that Morgan never sent him reinforcements at all and has abandoned him. Enraged and hurt by this supposed betrayal, Woodwose goes to attack Morgan. During the chaos at the throne room, Aurora incites an angry mob to kill Morgan.

Shortly after Cnoc na Riabh is crowned the new queen, Aurora poisons her.

She is later fatally stabbed by Melusine after the latter realized that allowing her to escape to Proper Human History would turn her into a monster and deprive her of the love she craved. Aurora watches Melusine in her dragon form helping Chaldea escape the collapsing British Lostbelt and realizes in her final thoughts that raising Melusine was the one good thing she did with her life.

ABILITIES

Aurora states that not even she could defeat Morgan if she faced her directly. In fact, she claims that Morgan could wipe the entire Wing Clan with one flick of her finger.

She has the ability to turn others into bugs.
