Coral (コーラル ?) is Wind Clan fairy residing in Salisbury in the British Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

BACKGROUND

Coral is the assistant to Aurora.

PERSONALITY

While her attitude towards human may be harsh, she had shown a strong interest in human culture as well.

FAE ROUND TABLE DOMAIN: AVALON LE FAE

Coral was turned into a bug for questioning orders and squashed by Aurora.

ABILITIES

She has the ability to turn others into rabbits.
