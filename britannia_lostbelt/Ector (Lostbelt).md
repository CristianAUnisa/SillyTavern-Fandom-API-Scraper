Ector (エクター?) is a fairy resident of the British Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

BACKGROUND

Ector was an Earth Clan Fairy, who was the companion of Aesc the Savior and later raised Artoria Caster in Tintagel. During his days with Aesc, he was known as the Black Knight (黒騎士?) and Ector the Immortal. His prime as a warrior long behind him, he lives his days as a humble blacksmith.

CREATION AND CONCEPTION

Ector was designed by nekotawawa in Fate/Grand Order.
