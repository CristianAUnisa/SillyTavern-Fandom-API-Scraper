Winky (ウィンキー ?) is a Fairy of Britain appearing in the English Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

FAE ROUND TABLE DOMAIN: AVALON LE FAE

Wag, Rob, and Winky find Mash after she lost her memories. After discussing what to do with her, they decide to sell her to Boggart when she defends them from a pack of Black Dogs, mistakenly believing she is the Child of Prophecy.

Upon arriving in Sheffield, Boggart accuses them of lying since he can tell Mash is human and orders them to be hanged. However after Mash defends them, he changes his mind and decides to pay them what they asked.

Winky later sold Sheffield out to Beryl Gut and Morgan's army. He is killed after Beryl interrogates him about Mash.
