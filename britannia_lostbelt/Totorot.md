Totorot (トトロット, Totorotto?), later going by the name Habetrot (ハべトロット, Habetorotto?), is a resident of the British Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

BACKGROUND

Makes her first appearance in the interlude as a thread-spinning fairy. Along with the Saviour Aesc, she puts an end to the conflict in Britain.

Her fate was greatly changed by her encounter with Mash, who was Rayshifted to the year 400 of the Fairy Calendar...

Or rather, she is a fairy who overcame her fate with all her might.

After the Saviour Aesc and the First Fairy Knight disappeared from the front stage, she toured around Britain by herself, spreading the culture about wedding ceremonies and sending out many brides.

By the time the era changed into the Queen Calendar, she turned herself into stone inside a twisted hole in the northern territories and continued to sleep until the time of meeting in the year 2017. After helping out Mash in Sheffield with an innocent look, she guided Chaldea's Master to Orkney and came to accept her own choices.

Once went by the name Totorot in the Lostbelt, but after being taught about the folklore of Habetrot from Proper Human History by Mash, she admired that way of being and started to call herself Habetrot upon waking up from her sleep of stone.

Originally a rambunctious kid from the Wing Clan, she began travelling together with Aesc as a comrade after losing in a duel.

(A thread-spinning fairy by nature, but she was troubled by the "disparity in her way of being" created by the fact that she couldn't send out human brides in the Lostbelt. That feeling of loss turned Totorot into a rambunctious kid).

After that, she thrived as the First Fairy Knight Totorot who fought together with the Saviour Aesc.

Incidentally, the one who wrote Mash's name in her shield with chalk during the interlude was Habetrot. After borrowing the shield while saying "show it to me", she quickly wrote "Mash" on it. The fact it was done in chalk (which is used for measurements) serves as proof.

FAE ROUND TABLE DOMAIN: AVALON LE FAE

Previously known as Totorot, the Knight of Beginnings, Habetrot was an ally of Aesc the Savior in the past. In the present, she is a seamstress working for Boggart. She befriends Mash Kyrielight.

Artoria's final attack evaporates 90% of the curses layered on Cernunnos and damages its body enough to reveal its Divine Core. Mash despairs they lack the means to destroy it before the flesh regenerates, though. But then Habetrot arrives; it is revealed that Aesc erased her and Habetrot's memories of Mash and Mash's memories of Habetrot to ensure they weren't erased as a paradox come the Queen's Calendar. Habetrot returns the Black Barrel, which she took from Sheffield before, to Mash. But due to Mash remembering her, she is erased as a paradox. Mash then uses the Black Barrel to destroy Cernunnos' Divine Core, ending the Great Calamity. With his duty complete, Cú Chulainn disappears, calling himself Chaldea's sole ally from the Proper History.

CREATION AND CONCEPTION
