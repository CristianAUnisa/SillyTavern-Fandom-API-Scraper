Britomart (ブリトマート, Buritomāto?), Class Name Lancer (ランサー, Ransā?), is a Lancer-class Servant summoned by Ritsuka Fujimaru in the Grand Orders of Fate/Grand Order.

IDENTITY

She is the second generation Britomart, named after her mother. Her mother was a fairy and her father was a human.

High Queen Morgan was spotted at the stargazer's fortress. Hearing the rumor from paradise, the second Britomart headed to Chaldea herself and encountered Morgan. Both impressed and excited, Britomart blurted out: "Please make me Your Majesty's Faerie Knight on your expeditions!" Morgan responded to this by building a massive athletic complex!

Before Britomart stood myriad obstacles and a horde of Servants who assembled to compete with each other! She was told she could become a Faerie Knight should she manage to overcome all the challenges before her. But she was also warned that one failure, one wrong move could be lethal! In fact, it was almost certain to be! Surely, such a prospect would bring Britomart to tears, but she defeated all the challenges and became was granted the title of expatriate Faerie Knight.

APPEARANCE

PERSONALITY

A proud, upright, and direct knightess with two distinct sides. According to her mother, she looks “just like I did when I was younger”. Therefore, she carries on the spirit of Britomart, a knightess who spent a good deal of her life adventuring and doing battle. Stand against the strong and care for the weak. If out in the world someone is about to lose their love, daringly rush in to save them. She is a proud knight by nature.

Laughing often and crying often. She’s seriously sensitive.

To her Master is both her "Lord" and a "traveling companion" who she shares daily adventures with.

CLASS SKILLS

 * Magic Resistance (B+ Rank): Invalidates magic with three verses or less. Receives mostly no damage even against greater magecraft or Greater Rituals. Depending on the situation, even greater magecraft would be completely invalidated.

PERSONAL SKILLS

 * Virtue of Chastity (A Rank): A virtue inherited from her mother, the original Britomart. Be it love or chastity, on adventures concerning such virtuous fidelity, the Faerie Knight Britomart will shine brilliantly with constant bonus values.

 * Fairy Knight (Faerieland) (C Rank): A skill that signifies her as a Faerie Knight under the Faerie Queen Gloriana. (The concept of 'Fairy Knight’ here is derived from Pan-Human History, and although similar to the "Fairy Knights" of the Lostbelt, it is ultimately a different thing.) Essentially, it has the effect of a low-ranked “Incarnated Elemental” skill. The Faerie Knight Britomart draws up mana from the earth and converts a certain amount of it into her own magical resources.

 * Mana Burst (Wind) (B Rank): A variant of the "Mana Burst" skill. The release of Britomart’s magical energy takes the form of wind. When this skill is activated, fairy wings temporarily form on her back. (Fundamentally not used in Fate/Grand Order)

NOBLE PHANTASM

Britomart's Noble Phantasms are Penetrate Bladud and Fortress Angela.
