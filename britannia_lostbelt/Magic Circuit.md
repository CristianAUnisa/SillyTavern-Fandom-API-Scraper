# Magic Circuit

Magic Circuits (魔術回路(マジック・サーキット), Majutsu Kairo(Majikku Sākitto)?) are a pseudo-nervous system that spreads itself through the human body and what qualifies a person to be a magus.

Magic Circuits have two basic functions; one is to convert Life Force (生命力, Inochiryoku?) into magical energy and another is to allow their owner to access Magecraft Foundations (魔術基盤, Majutsu Kiban?).

MECHANISMS

Magic Circuits are paths that connect the material world to the astral plane. To perform Mysteries, a magus connects his Magic Circuits to a Magecraft Foundation, emit a command and dispatch magical energy to fuels the execution of the magecraft written within one of the Magic Formulae (魔術式, Majutsu Shiki?) included in the Magecraft Foundation as if someone switched a computer on and sends it commands to run a computer program, a process described as simultaneous.

Magic Circuits reside within the magus’ soul, and what is found on the body is just a physical expression of them that stretches itself throughout the body in a fashion similar to the nervous system. They are composed of core components, which are the actual Magic Circuits so to say, and bypasses that connect those components to the brain. Magic Circuits do not change, and once damaged, they cannot be repaired.

Because organic activity is necessary for the operation of Magic Circuits, it is a common mistake to think that they are literally a part of the magus body. There are rare occasions in which the Magic Circuits continue working even after the practitioner’s body has ceased all vital functions. In these cases, destruction of the brain is necessary to stop the Magic Circuits. Their Quality (質, Shitsu?) and Quantity (量, Ryō?) are ranked through letters like magecraft, and their Composition (編成, Hensei?), that describes the overall ability, often varies between magi.

Like any other organ, the number of Magic Circuits that one possesses is determined at birth and cannot decrease or increase naturally, meaning that those from older lineages will be more powerful, but it is possible to change that number artificially through a number of methods. One of those methods is through the establishment of a mystic pass between two magi from which the Magic Circuits can transferred. As the Magic Circuits are located in the soul, a fusion of body and mind is required during the transplant. However, the process carries the same risks and complications as an organ transplant. Furthermore, as the Magic Circuits become unstable if tampered with while in use, there is a possibility of mutual destruction if a contract similar to that between a magus and his familiar has not been established beforehand.

For that and other reasons, lineages of magi do not seek to tamper with the Magic Circuits of their members while they are alive, but rather try to produce descendants with the greatest "extra" number of Magic Circuits possible. Note that number is not everything, and quality and precision of the Magic Circuits are also an important factor that differs from lineage to lineage. The Aozaki bloodline is known for producing magi with a low quantity of Magic Circuits, but the great quality of their Magic Circuits is more than enough to compensate for that. Meanwhile, the Barthomeloi possess a powerful brand of Magic Circuits known as Blue Blood: Noble Magic Circuits (貴い魔術回路(ブルーブラッド), Tōtoi Majutsu Kairo(Burū Buraddo)?).

A normal person rarely acquires Magic Circuits but in these cases they are considered as sudden mutations. While Kirei Kotomine was born with sudden Magic Circuits not of his lineage, he is not of this type. Kirei's Magic Circuits were a gift from the divine sacrament, rewarding his father, Risei Kotomine, for years of pious worship. Kirei was born with "the right to recreate the miracles in the divine sacrament". This "right", in other words, was the Magic Circuits. It is also possible to be born with atrophied Magic Circuits such as in the case of Shinji Matou. These atrophied Magic Circuits would require the magical equivalent a nuclear fusion reactor to be implanted in their user to open; this can be achieved with the core of a Lesser Grail, in can be used to forcibly open atrophied Magic Circuits in order to use the user as a new vessel for the Holy Grail.

Known numbers of Magic Circuits by characters

Still, the Magic Circuits are ultimately something unnatural for the human body and their activity will cause pain and numbness to the magus. Their very presence in the human body is something uncommon and an ordinary person will have little to none. Also, the use of Magic Circuits increases the Magus bodily temperature, which is what allowed Kiritsugu Emiya to discover enemy Masters on the battlefield through the employment of a thermal sensor during the Fourth Grail War.

Magic Circuits are "opened" when magical energy is run through them for the first time. Based on their experience at that time, a magus makes their own mental trigger to activate their Magic Circuits at will from then on. For this reason, the triggers are different from each other and in an extreme case, self-injury can be regarded as a switch. An unexpectedly large number of magi have sexual excitement as their triggers and moreover many of them rely on drugs.

Known mental triggers by characters

Prior to the Fifth Holy Grail War, Shirou believed that he had to "make" a new Magic Circuit every time he wanted to perform magecraft. In each attempt to do so, he temporarily (and inadvertently) converted parts of his nerves into makeshift Magic Circuits. A very dangerous procedure, as failure would result in his body breaking down from the inside out and even with success the resulting pain would be comparable to inserting a hot iron rod into the spine. Furthermore, when his natural born Magic Circuits were finally opened, his nervous system went haywire due the sudden intake of magical energy. At first, neither of his teachers of magecraft (Kiritsugu and Rin Tohsaka) noticed that habit of his.

A lesser known function of Magic Circuits is that they can be used to record and store information, in a manner not too dissimilar from computers or smartphones, like replaying scenes seen in one's head. Any magus that is at least somewhat capable use them as a calculator in addition to their brains when solving problems. They can also be used as a precise internal clock to keep track of time. Some magi use their Magic Circuits to compute translations. Whether it's calculation or communication, Magic Circuits can do as much processing as a mobile terminal can do.

As demonstrated by Fiore Forvedge Yggdmillennia and Kayneth El-Melloi Archibald, it is possible for Magic Circuits to negatively interfere with the user's body, either through birth or through outside interference.

In Fiore's case, she was born with defective Magic Circuits in both of her legs, and this deficiency not only profoundly affects her magecraft, it also caused both limbs to stop working, causing her excruciating pain at times. The only possible way of healing would be through removing the Magic Circuits in her legs. In other words, it would mean giving up the path of a magus.

In Kayneth's situation, he fell victim to Kiritsugu Emiya's Origin Bullet which destroyed his Magic Circuits, rendering him unable to practice magecraft. The fact that he survived in his debilitating state was due to his quick attention by Sola-Ui Nuada-Re Sophia-Ri, who restored his internal organs, but the muscles and nerves were irreparably damaged. At first, he was completely paralyzed, with no hope of walking again. It took a large sum of money and a Puppet maker to get only his hands in working order, and that only allowed him to move within the confines of a wheelchair.

Reines El-Melloi Archisorte has a condition where her Mystic Eyes quickly overheat when they come into contact with magical energy, to the point where the burning sensation is compared to an allergic reaction, thanks to the immaturity of her brain and Magic Circuits. The development of her brain and Magic Circuits did not catch up with that of her Mystic Eyes, causing them to overreact in response to their use. However, as a side effect, it is due to this overreaction that her accuracy in magecraft has become prominent.

It is possible for magi to connect to the Magic Circuits of others; however, this is considered insanity. By connecting to someone's Magic Circuits, the receiver of the connection had absolute control. For a magus with mediocre ability, the receiver would easily be able to manipulate, and even burn, the Magic Circuits of the connected magus. Considering how Magic Circuits are linked to the body's nerves, it was similar to a magus placing his living heart into the hands of the magus receiver. With the connection, one of the effects was that a magus could flow their own magical energy into the receiver of the connection.

EQUIVALENTS

 * Magical Energy Reactor

Differing from the Magic Circuits of regular humans, Artoria Pendragon and Siegfried when he was alive have a Magic Core (魔術炉心, Majutsu Roshin?) granted by their Dragon blood. Its capacity is far greater than any human can achieve. While a magus' body can be considered a machine which creates magical energy, Artoria's Magic Core acts as a factory which creates magical energy.

Gray also gets a Dragon Magic Core after Artoria is summoned as Saber in the Fifth Holy Grail War, with the summoning affecting Gray's body due to her nature, and turning her more into a perfect clone of Artoria.

For Divine Patterns, see Fairy.

REGRESSION TO THE AGE OF GODS

Beings like Lugh Beowulf, Arcueid Brunestud and Tiamat have Regression to the Age of Gods (神代回帰, Jindai Kaiki?, also called Reversion to the Age of Gods) as their own analogue of Magic Circuits. It represents the extent at which they are capable of producing Mysteries that existed on Earth before Magic. They are ranked with quantifiable letters like Magic Circuits.

Their Quality ranking represents "how distant from what humans are capable of" when reproducing their pure Mysteries. Their Quantity ranking represents power, the higher the quantity, the greater the degree of influence over the surrounding environment. The C-rank Quantity allows for the display of power over a village at most. Their Composition represents the era of the "Mystery", such as Runes, Kabbalah, and Black Magic do so in magecraft. While similar in concept, things like Alice Kuonji's Ploys are not from the Age of Gods and cannot be assigned a number in Regression to the Age of Gods.
