Halobaromia (ハロバロミア, Harobaromia?) is a figure appearing in the English Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

BACKGROUND

Halobaromia is a member of the Wind Clan.

APPEARANCE

He had wings but Aurora had them removed.

PERSONALITY

Aurora described him as argumentative.

FAE ROUND TABLE DOMAIN: AVALON LE FAE

When the fairies start fighting over who gets Ritsuka, Halobaromia tries to calm everyone down. Unfortunately, he and the rest of his clan are killed by the Earth Clan.

Later it is revealed that some time ago he was exiled from Salisbury by Aurora, for whom he worked as an advisor preceding Coral, but eventually Aurora got annoyed with him because "was always arguing with [her] with his silly logic".
