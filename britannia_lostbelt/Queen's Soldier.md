Queen's Soldier (女王兵, Joō-Hei?) later known as Morgan's Soldier (モルガン兵, Morugan-Hei?) are the military force of Queen Morgan in the British Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

CHARACTERISTICS

In Fate/Grand Order, they appear as enemies, introduced in the British Lostbelt.

Queen's Soldier's Saint Graph Designation are Caster in Fate/Grand Order.
