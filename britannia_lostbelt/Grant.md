Knight Grant (騎士グラント?) is a Fang Clan Fairy of Britain serving as one of the Queen's Soldiers of Morgan in the British Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

BACKGROUND

After being ostracized due to being the only survivor of a battle, he was discovered by Morgan and became a knight. Loyal to Morgan and wishing to serve in Camelot, he changed from serving in the castle to managing the human farm west of Salisbury after being defeated by Percival in the Tournament of Robins.
