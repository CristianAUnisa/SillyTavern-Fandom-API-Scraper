Onpham (オンファム, Onfamu?) is a figure appearing in the English Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

BACKGROUND

Onpham is a member of the Earth Clan.

FAE ROUND TABLE DOMAIN: AVALON LE FAE

When the fairies start fighting over who gets Ritsuka, Onpham and his fellow clansmen massacre the Wind Clan.
