Redra Bbit (レッドラ・ビット, Redora Bitto?) is a Fairy of the Fang Clan appearing in the English Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

APPEARANCE

Redra Bbit is identical in appearance to Servant Red Hare.

FAE ROUND TABLE DOMAIN: AVALON LE FAE

Redra Bbit serves Aurora and helps Chaldea get around the Fairy Kingdom by pulling their carriage.
