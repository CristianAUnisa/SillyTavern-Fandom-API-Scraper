Mike (マイク?) is a Fairy of the Earth Clan appearing in the English Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

BACKGROUND

Mike runs a tavern in Salisbury. He hires Da Vinci as a waitress after she gets separated from the rest of her group in the Nameless Forest.

PERSONALITY

Like most fairies, Mike only has a surface-level understanding of human culture despite living in cities modeled after the ones humans use. He didn't know that the rooms on the second floor of his tavern were supposed to be inn rooms for guests, and he didn't know you could use flour to bake bread as fairies can simply conjure bread out of thin air. Da Vinci had to explain both of these to him.

He refers to Da Vinci as "Duhbinchi".

FAE ROUND TABLE DOMAIN: AVALON LE FAE

During the chaos of his Lostbelt's final days, he genuinely considers stabbing Da Vinci to death so she won't leave him to rejoin Chaldea, but unlike many of his fellow fae, resists such a selfish and violent impulse.
