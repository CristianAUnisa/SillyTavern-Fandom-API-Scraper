# Magical Energy

Magical energy (魔力, Maryoku?) is a source of energy found naturally within magi and the Earth, acting as the source to actualize the Mysteries of magecraft.

CHARACTERISTICS

Referred to as the primordial animus (原初の生命力, Gensho no Inochiryoku?) and life itself (命そのもの, Inochi Sonomono?), magical energy is the source that powers all forms of magecraft. Analogous to gasoline, it is an essential element for the invocation of spells. There have been many names for magical energy depending on the culture.

Magical energy is primarily classified by two types, mana and od, separated only by whether it comes from the atmosphere or from within human magi. They are essentially identical in terms of quality, but the world's mana vastly exceeds humanity's od.

In the Holy Church, it is considered taboo to use the terminology preferred by magi due to the organization’s long-standing conflict with the Mage’s Association. Members of the Church therefore refer to magical energy as "Holy Energy" (祈りの力, Inori no Chikara?, localized as "Power of Prayer").

Magical energy can be absorbed from others. Gray, using the scythe form of her unique Mystic Code, Add, it is able to absorb magical energy, and in the Fuyuki City's Fifth Holy Grail War, Medea absorbed the od of civilians through the leylines to provide herself with an unlimited supply of magical energy.

MANA

Mana (大源(マナ), Daigen(Mana)?) is the greater source of magical energy found in the atmosphere of the Earth. The meaning of mana is approximately the same as that of Polynesian culture.

Mana is the "breath of the Planet filling the world and nature," produced by the Planet Earth through a network of Leylines (龍脈, Ryū Myaku?, Dragon Vein) across the globe. There also exist alternate versions of leylines, such as the Divine-make Leylines (神造霊脈, Jinzō Rei Myaku?, God-made Spiritual Vein) of Olympus, a wireless network created and administrated solely by the Gods, capable of superior mana provision compared to regular leylines.

The density of mana on the planet is dependent on the age. One of the main factors that defined the Age of Gods was its magical energy. During that time, the atmosphere was full of artificial mana known as True Ether. Furthermore, the mana was not only more more abundant than in modern day, but more potent as well. Within that Age, the mana density is such that a modern human will die when exposed to it without a specialized Mystic Code to block out the mana. The reason for that is because humanity has adapted to an environment where magical energy is vanishing, and their bodies can no longer withstand their original one now that they parted from the World of Magecraft. As such, to people of the modern age, magical energy from the Age of the Gods is basically a poison, and too much of it can damage internal organs. Since the start of the A.D. era, however, the magical energy in the atmosphere has been steadily decreasing.

The mana within a given area is finite, with the three-kilometer cavern housing the Fuyuki Greater Holy Grail holding one thousand units. Similar to magi needing time to regenerate their od, the mana of an area will take a long time to replenish after it is depleted.

Magi freely utilize the mana in the air for the purpose of spells that exceed their personal od, but the amount they can utilize at one time correlates to the capacity of their Magic Circuits. If there is no available mana, they can only utilize small-scale spells with their own storage. It is also possible for magi to interfere with leylines, such as the technique of the Native American tribe of the Protectors of the Land, which consists of having promising newborn's Magic Circuits forcibly linked to the land's leylines through a ritual requiring human sacrifice. This connection can help to strengthen senses, or convert the mana gushing from the leylines into od through one's Magic Circuits. The major drawback of this method is that if the magus physically leaves the land, they will die. Karabo Frampton has been shown to be able to utilize his Black Keys to connect to and activate leylines by placing them at focal points, and Doctor Heartless connected the leylines of Japan with the leylines of London to reproduce the structure of the Holy Grail War.

The Forest of Einnashe directly controls its atmosphere, so magi cannot use the mana from environment. Satsuki Yumizuka's Reality Marble Depletion Garden rapidly depletes the mana in the area, acting as a natural enemy to magi and Elementals. Within the World of Fate/EXTRA, worldwide Mana Depletion (魔力枯渇, Maryoku Kokatsu?) brings about the end of magecraft, many magi becoming Wizards.

In the Spiritual Tomb of Albion lies the Great Magic Circuit (大魔術回路, Dai Majutsu Kairo?), otherwise known as Vein Corridor Od Vena (静脈回廊オドベナ, Jōmyaku Kairō Odobena?). It is Britain's leyline cavern. Magic Circuits are fused with the earth here but cannot be harmed by the excavating magi, and so the excavations avoid them, forming various passages like corridors. This layer is inhabited by Magical Beasts, and the Magic Circuits are said to lead one to the Reverse Side of the World. It is unknown if the Magic Circuits are from the Dragon of Albion, or if they already existed as a part of the earth.

OD

Od (小源(オド), Shōgen(Odo)?), from the concept of Odic force, is the life force (生命力, Inochiryoku?) energy found within living organisms such as humans, animals and Magical Beasts. Once depleted, the Magic Circuits that are stored in the human soul will begin to replenish it. Od is produced as long as one lives, but the amount a person can store varies from individual to individual. By the 21st Century, magi power their Mystery through the od produced by their Magic Circuits alone.

The difference between mana and od is their abundance. Mana can be found almost anywhere and in great quantities, thus it is called the Greater Source (大源, Daigen?) by the magi. As od is the energy inside humans and animals, it is much smaller in amount and is called the Lesser Source (小源, Shōgen?).

Strictly speaking, mana and od are energy in its raw state, whereas magical energy is the name given to the energy once it is converted by the Magic Circuits. Regarding nature interference magecraft mentioned before, at least for large-scale spells, activation requires the use of mana regardless of the amount of od that the magus has. Magical energy itself does not have any elemental attribute. However, once embedded into an object that does have said attribute (such as jewels) or that is under the influence of Nature Spirits, it will attune itself to the Elements in question until the moment that it is released.

The magical energy itself is held only inside the body fundamentally and if released in the outer world, it will return to the life force and be scattered into the Greater Source. This has also been expressed as Evaporation (気化, Kika?). If someone has Magic Circuits and they are still operating, an individual's od will be regenerated even if is totally used up. However, without Magic Circuits, od will no longer be generated at all.

For magi, their own bodies makes for first-rate catalysts. For example, it has always been easy to turn human hair or eyes into raw material useful for magecraft: the purer the eye color, the better, and the longer the hair, the better. Hair that has been cultivated and maintained over many years becomes an especially powerful and precious fuel and raw material. Long hair can be used for many purposes, like an instant Reinforcement spell, transcribing Magic Formulae, making Familiars, creating a Chain of Submission able to restrain a Phantasmal Species, and amplifying a Mystic Code among other things. The importance of long hair is especially important for women, who have strong magical power residing in their hair seen as the symbol of their magical powers and as an extension and "copy" of the female magus. Due to this, they use their hair as a secret weapon and trump card equivalent to Mystic Codes, and ribbons used to tie them are suitable magical artifacts, as holding the hair in place means holding the magical energy in place. Even if men accumulate magical energy in their hair, it is not as effective as women. Magical energy is also prone to merge with the fluids (such as blood or semen) of the magus, and these fluids are good at holding in magical energy for some time even after leaving the body. Because of this, by drinking a magus' blood, one can replenish one's magical energy. Magi with monetary problems have also been said to sell their bodily fluids, in which magical energy has been merged, to the Mage's Association. And in ancient Egypt, there existed the practice of sewing one's eyelids together to prevent magical energy from escaping the body.

Qi (気, Ki?, localized as "Chi") is one of the culturally-specific names of od. Li Shuwen uses qi for his Sphere Boundary and No Second Strike techniques.

KNOWN MAGICAL ENERGY LEVELS

There is some inconsistency between the various visual novels that make up the Nasuverse. In Fate/stay night, the average amount of od is 25 units of magical energy. However, in Tsukihime, the average amount of od is 40 units of magical energy.

OTHER FORMS OF MAGICAL ENERGY

Aside from life force, whether from the Planet or one's self, there are many other forms that magical energy can take. It can be known as spiritual power, willpower, vitality, survival instinct, dan t'ian in Chinese thinking, kundalini in India, prana, etc. Some use their force of will, and there are some that use magecraft by paying a price other than themselves. Replenishment of spiritual calories by consuming them as food can also give increased spiritual power to the soul, as can the desire to leave behind descendants, i.e. sexual activities.

There is also significant increase in land's mana during festivals and holidays, with such charged distortions being a boon to a magus' craft, be it creative or destructive. The Tower of London emits what the Clock Tower considers the "magical energy of the dead", which consists of the large amounts of mana seeping from the leylines and the od given off unintentionally by the tourists, with the "concept" of the dead at its core.

MANA CRYSTAL

Mana Crystals (マナの結晶, Mana no Kesshō?), also known as Ete Amorpha (血体格子(けったいこう), Chi-tai Kōshi(Ketta Ikou)?), are crystals of magical energy created through human sacrifice. True Archer described them as resembling the mystic crystals used by jewel magi, but seeming several orders of magnitude more pure.

Known to Greek witches, a technique to create them was developed by the Galliasta family, using exchange in the form of a "primitive curse" to generate magical energy from human sacrifices, and consisting of a ritual that would take a month and require a transmutation Incantation of a three-day and night duration. Atrum Galliasta modernized and sped up the process, skipping the incantation process entirely, having the sacrifices placed in cylinders, liquefied, and then transferred into a crystal. It is more efficient with "pure" raw materials, so he used young girls as sacrifices.

This system would later be improved and expanded to a far larger scale by the Scladio Family, able to use dozens of sacrifices simultaneously.

MANA PRISM

Mana Prisms (マナプリズム, Mana Purizumu?) are crystals created through concentrating mana.

RARE PRISM

Rare Prisms (レアプリズム, Rea Purizumu?) are rare crystals created by concentrating mana under special circumstances.

QUANTUM PIECE

Quantum Pieces (クォンタムピース, Kuontamu Pīsu?), abbreviated as QP, are a magical energy resource formed from the spacial magical occupancy value, the quantum of the Magecraft Foundation.

Due to the nature of their usage in the gameplay of Fate/Grand Order, QP is treated as the basic form of currency related to Servants, especially in comedic settings.
