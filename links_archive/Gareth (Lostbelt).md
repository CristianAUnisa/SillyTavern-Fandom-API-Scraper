Gareth (ガレス?) is a Mirror Clan Fairy of Britain appearing in the British Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

BACKGROUND

Gareth is the successor of Ainsel. Gareth didn't initially know her heritage, but ever since she was born she has been wandering with a purpose in her heart. She wished to become a knight who protects people.

FAE ROUND TABLE DOMAIN: AVALON LE FAE

After witnessing Artoria's confrontation with Fairy Knight Tristan in Gloucester, Gareth is so impressed by her that she grows determined to become Artoria's squire and starts following Artoria's group across Britain.

It is later revealed that Gareth is the reincarnation of Ainsel.

ABILITIES

Due to being the next Ainsel, Gareth has been granted certain abilities such as foresight to certain events or seeing through Knight Portunes’ invisibility.

SKILLS

 * Squire of Prophecy (予言の従者?) (C Rank): Functionally the same as The Wolf Never Sleeps.
 * Intuitive Gareth (勘の良いガレス?) (B Rank): Functionally the same as Gareth Beaumains.
 * Knight's Stance (騎士の構え?) (B Rank): Functionally the same as Ring of Transformation.
