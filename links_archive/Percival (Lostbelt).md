Percival (パーシヴァル?) is a human leading the Round Table Army in the British Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

BACKGROUND

Percival in this chapter is the alternate version from the British Lostbelt. Percival leads the Round Table Army of human and fairy rebels against Morgan. His forces are based in Londinium.

Percival was born on one of the Fairy Kingdom's human farms and grew up in Salisbury, where he and other children were treated well and trained as soldiers to fight the Moss. He regards Fairy Knight Lancelot as his older sister, as the two of them were raised and trained together.

He is called Percival of the White Light (白光のパーシヴァル?), the pinnacle of humanity. He participated in the games at Camelot and was even able to survive against Fairy Knight Lancelot, which impressed Morgan so much that she ordered him to join her army despite being human. Percival defied the queen and went to lead the rebel army instead. Although the army was actually rallied and founded by Oberon, and Percival personally sees Oberon as the leader.

Percival later admits to Artoria that he, and the other children, were being raised as substitutes for the Child of Prophecy in case one didn't appear. After being chosen to wield the Spear of Selection and using it for the first time, Percival aged 10 years. At the time that Ritsuka Fujimaru enters the British Lostbelt, Percival is 16 years old inhabiting a 26 year old body.

COMBAT

Tam Lin Lancelot's physical capabilities, Magical Energy level, and time, surpass Percival in every way. However, Percival managed to put a good fight against her during the "Tournament of Robin" making Morgan claim he had proved himself equal to a Tam Lin. During the attack in the Welsh Woods, Percival fights Melusine again. This time, Melusine couldn't fly as fast as she normally does due to all the trees getting in her way. Percival claims that is the only reason he could hold out against her as long as he did. Even with this drawback, she was still too fast for Human eyes to follow. While Percival could anticipate her moves, he was too slow to guard against them. However, Percival was still durable enough to take at least one of her hits, and Melusine was surprised that his arm was still attached afterward. In the night before the assault on Camelot, Percival admitted that while he may not be able to defeat her, he swears to at least keep her busy until the battle is over. After Cnoc na Riabh's coronation, he also claimed that he could handle her on his own. After Melusine withdrew, Percival said that he was taking a calculated risk, and that he doesn't know what we would have done if that hadn't worked.

During the British Lostbelt, Super Mash and fatigued Percival were able to make Dragon of Albion's Remnant withdraw.

NOBLE PHANTASM

His Noble Phantasm is Lost Longinus. According to Woodwose, Percival would have defeated Tam Lin Lancelot during the Tournament of Robins had he used Lost Longinus. When Dragon of Albion's Remnant tried to withdraw from battle, Percival decided to finish it off. Although the Dragon struck him several times, Percival was able to counterattack and strike it with Lost Longinus. This attack pierced Albion's heart and knocked it out from the sky.
