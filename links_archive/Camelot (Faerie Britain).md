Camelot (キャメロット?) is the capital of Faerie Britain in the British Lostbelt.

CHARACTERISTICS

The Camelot in the British Lostbelt is a giant Mystic Code designed by Morgan that was built using the power of the Faerie Clans. Even though the Spell to make it was utterly massive, its connections to her Magic Circuits and Magical Energy circulation are absolutely perfect. Due to this, Artoria Caster regards it as the work of a prodigy without equal. Mash Kyrielight had seen many Magical Reactor Cores before in the sixth lostbelt, but Camelot's easily surpasses them all. Da Vinci agrees, saying it is both the Ends of the World, and its center. She claims that it is just as strong as the Tower of the Ends of the World from the Sixth Singularity. Totorot also states that the Magical Energy in the city is like nowhere else in Faerie Britain. In fact, Morgan has so much mana that she's not even worried about Cnoc na Riabh's 30,000 troops, who are beneath their notice. There is enough magical energy to cover all of Faerie Britain. Morgan claims that she has nothing to fear from the Great Calamity Cernunnos due to all the Magical Energy she has stored in the throne.
