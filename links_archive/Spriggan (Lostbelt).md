Lord Spriggan (スプリガン様?) is a changeling human posing as an Earth Clan fairy acting as Head of the Earth Clan (土の氏族の長?) and Lord of Norwich (ノリッジの領主?) in the British Lostbelt in Fate/Grand Order.

BACKGROUND

His previous name was Capless (キャップレス?). He was originally a human from Proper Human History, a Japanese man who lived during the Edo Period under the surname "Nakamura". He was a sailor visiting Britain who fell into the English Lostbelt as a Changeling, the term used for people and objects who find their way into Fairy Britain from other worlds. Capless worked with the original Spriggan, but eventually betrayed and killed him then stole his name.

Spriggan is the leader of the Earth Clan in the English Lostbelt. He is also the leader of Norwich. He took over Norwich from its previous ruler, Boggart, and banished Boggart and his fairies to the north. After that Spriggan monopolized the land in Norwich so that the new fairies who moved in only rented it; however, recently he has put Norwich up for sale and so others have bought it up. Immediately after this, the Calamity Pool appeared in the sky above the city.

Because of the prophecy, Spriggan knew that a Calamity would strike Norwich and kill the fairies present. After that, Norwich will become a vacant plot of land and he’ll gain the rights to it again. Spriggan obtains the proceeds from the land that was forced to be sold without having to work for it at all and then, he’ll also obtain the opportunity to redevelop Norwich at his own will.

ABILITIES

Spriggan had disguised himself as a fairy through cosmetic surgery, used perfume to disguise his smell, and used a Mystic Code to disguise his magical energy signature.

CREATION AND CONCEPTION

Spriggan was designed by Mineji Sakamoto in Fate/Grand Order.
